Question: has anyone published an out-of-sample test of the Ditlevsen & Ditlevsen (2023) AMOC tipping
model on sea-surface temperatures after 2020, or refitted its tipping year with data to 2024-2026?

2. "arXiv 2026 AMOC collapse early warning tipping time estimate HadISST updated through 2025": nothing;

   AMOC stability, no DD23 analysis).

6. "github Ditlevsen AMOC estimation HadISST 2021 ... 2025 refit tipping point": no code or refit found.

Closest prior work: Morr et al. 2026 (arXiv:2604.20341): penalty, coverage and fingerprint-choice
critique on data to 2021. Ben-Yami et al. 2024 (Science Advances, doi:10.1126/sciadv.adl4841):
uncertainty critique. Neither tests the model on later data.

New here: the first out-of-sample test of the published forecast (69 months, 2021-01..2026-09) with a
replica of the authors' own estimator that first reproduces their published numbers, and the refitted
tipping year as each new year of data is added.
