# Prior-art check, 2026-11-24 (before the job ran)

Question: has anyone tested the Ditlevsen & Ditlevsen (2023) AMOC tipping model on SST months after 2020, or
refitted it with them?

- arXiv API, `all:AMOC AND all:tipping`, newest 40 of 47 (to 2611.23429, 2026-11-17): no abstract re-estimates a
  tipping time with post-2020 SST, extends the SG-GM fingerprint, or forward-tests DD23.
- Morr, Ben-Yami, Groenke, Schoetz, Cotronei, Myrvoll-Nilsen, Bathiany, Rypdal & Boers, arXiv:2604.20341 (2026-04-22),
  full text: the most thorough re-analysis of DD23; uses "1870-2021 HadISST" and other products; no out-of-sample
  test and no fit with later SST months.
- Rahmstorf & Caesar, arXiv:2611.23429 (2026-11-17): a synthesis of evidence for AMOC slowing, focused south of
  45N; the abstract mentions no updated fingerprint fit or tipping time.
- Emirzade et al., arXiv:2609.18551: new optimised fingerprints; not a DD23 forward test.
- NPG 2025 (Bayesian nested time-dependent AR model): the same HadISST fingerprint, data to 2020.
- Web searches for an updated DD23 tipping year with 2021-2025 data: none found; press coverage still quotes the
  2023 numbers.
- DD23 Author Correction (Nat. Commun. 16, 7794, 2025-08-21): fixes the Strang-splitting estimator (identified by
  Anders Gantzhorn Kristensen); the tipping-year estimate changes by 8 years; the corrected article gives 2065
  (95%: 2037-2109) for SG - 2xGM. Same data, 1870-2020.

Re-check right before any article is published.
